# Research basis

## Motivation

Modern grids must balance demand with increasingly variable renewable supply. Changes in electricity consumption and weather-driven solar and wind output make accurate forecasts valuable for planning, renewable utilization, imbalance reduction, and reliable grid operation.

This project will study an MLOps lifecycle covering data processing, training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring for demand, solar, and wind forecasting. The intended contribution is governance-oriented: agentic assistance may suggest or prepare lifecycle actions, while deterministic checks and people control consequential promotion and rollback decisions.

## Research question

Can bounded Agentic AI reduce manual intervention in an MLOps forecasting lifecycle without giving the AI unrestricted authority over critical model decisions?
